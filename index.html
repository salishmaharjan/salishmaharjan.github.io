Research with a grid-scale view.

Large loads · EMT modeling

Data centers and grid dynamics

Developing parameterized electromagnetic transient models for data-center loads and benchmarking against PSS®E PERC1, with frequency scans for small-signal stability and interconnection studies.

DOE · Community microgrid

Renewable energy for rural Iowa

Led technical work on a renewable community microgrid with Montezuma Light & Power, including techno-economic analysis and pathways to resilient local energy.

DOE · RECHARGE

Resilience for charging networks

Connected weather, outage, feeder, and EV charging data to resilience metrics, restoration models, and investment planning for more reliable distribution grids.
