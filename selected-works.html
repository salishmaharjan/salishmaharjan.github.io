01 / Selected work

Research with a grid-scale view.

Engineering and research across emerging large loads, community energy, and distribution-grid restoration.

01

Data centers and grid dynamics

Large loads · EMT modeling · INS Engineering

Develop electromagnetic transient models for hyperscale data-center loads, including cooling towers, chillers, air-handling units, UPS systems, and server rooms. Benchmark the parameterized INS-ISU model against PSS®E PERC1, with frequency scans for small-signal stability and interconnection studies.

02

Renewable energy for rural Iowa

DOE-supported community microgrid · Montezuma Light & Power

Led technical work and techno-economic analysis for a rural community microgrid designed around a 2.5 MW solar array, 1.5 MWh battery storage, EV chargers, and AMI-enabled load control. New substation switchgear enables seamless islanding so the community can operate through transmission outages.

03

Resilience for charging networks

RECHARGE · Chicago, Indianapolis, and Ames

Worked with AES and Ames Municipal Utilities on EV charging and distribution resilience. Integrated weather sensors, outages, charging locations, feeders, fragility, and restoration-time metrics to guide storm preparation, grid-hardening investments, and future charger placement, with attention to disadvantaged communities.

04

Black start and dynamic microgrids

Distribution restoration · Grid-forming resources · Solar-HERO

Developed restoration methods for utility feeders using grid-forming and grid-following inverters, synchronization-aware switching, and dynamic microgrid formation. Work includes black-start planning with frequency-security constraints and MPC-based restoration for real utility feeders such as Connexus Energy and United Power.

05

Voltage sensitivity and decentralized control

Distribution grids · Smart inverters · Optimization

Developed analytical estimation methods for voltage, current, and loss sensitivities in unbalanced distribution systems, alongside decentralized voltage-control algorithms for distributed energy resources and closed-loop testing with dynamic models and state estimation.

06

Building energy predictive control

Buildings · IoT · Model predictive control

Built cloud-enabled predictive control for building energy systems integrating HVAC, storage, solar PV, and loads, with connected building sensors and energy-management systems.
