01 / Selected work

Research with a grid-scale view.

Engineering and research across emerging large loads, community energy, and distribution-grid restoration.

01

Data centers and grid dynamics

Large loads · EMT modeling · INS Engineering

Develop electromagnetic transient models for hyperscale data-center loads, including cooling towers, chillers, air-handling units, UPS systems, and server rooms. Benchmark the parameterized INS-ISU model against PSS®E PERC1, with frequency scans for small-signal stability and interconnection studies. Current work also examines large-load impacts on grid dynamics and system strength.

02

Renewable energy for rural Iowa

$12M DOE OCED award · Montezuma Light & Power

Led technical work and techno-economic analysis for a rural community microgrid designed around a 2.5 MW solar array, 1.5 MWh battery storage, EV chargers, and AMI-enabled load control. New substation switchgear is designed to enable seamless islanding during transmission outages, supporting local resilience, reliability, and sustainable operation.

03

Resilience for charging networks

$2M DOE VETO award · Chicago, Indianapolis, and Ames

Worked with AES and Ames Municipal Utilities on EV charging and distribution resilience. Integrated weather sensors, outages, charging locations, feeders, fragility, and restoration-time metrics to guide storm preparation, DER investment, grid hardening, and future charger placement, with attention to energy justice and disadvantaged communities.

04

Black start and dynamic microgrids

Distribution restoration · Grid-forming resources · Solar-HERO

Developed short-term and real-time restoration methods for utility feeders using grid-forming and grid-following inverters, synchronizing switches, synchronization-enabled linear power flow, and dynamic microgrid formation. Solar-HERO work includes black-start planning with frequency-security constraints and MPC-based restoration for real utility feeders such as Connexus Energy and United Power.

05

Voltage sensitivity and decentralized control

Distribution grids · Smart inverters · Optimization

Developed analytical estimation methods for voltage, current, and loss sensitivities in unbalanced distribution systems with smart inverters. Related control work includes decentralized voltage regulation for distributed energy resources and robust control tested in closed loop with dynamic models and state estimation.

06

Building energy predictive control

Buildings · IoT · Model predictive control

Built cloud-enabled predictive real-time control for building energy systems integrating HVAC, storage, solar PV, and loads. Connected the control algorithm with building systems and sensors through an Internet of Things platform; use cases include demand response and time-of-use energy management.
