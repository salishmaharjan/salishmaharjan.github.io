01 / Selected work

Research with a grid-scale view.

Engineering and research across emerging large loads, community energy, and distribution-grid restoration.

01

Data centers and grid dynamics

Large loads · EMT modeling · INS Engineering

Develop electromagnetic transient models for large data-center loads and benchmark the parameterized INS-ISU model against PSS®E PERC1. Work includes frequency scans for small-signal stability analysis and interconnection studies for large loads and inverter-based resources.

02

Renewable energy for rural Iowa

DOE-supported community microgrid · Montezuma Light & Power

Led technical work on a renewable community microgrid, coordinating with the local utility and conducting techno-economic analysis to explore a resilient, locally grounded energy system.

03

Resilience for charging networks

RECHARGE · Utility resilience · DOE-supported

Developed geospatial and resilience analyses for EV charging stations and distribution systems, integrating weather sensors, outages, charging locations, feeder data, fragility metrics, restoration times, and investment planning.

04

Black start and dynamic microgrids

Distribution restoration · Grid-forming resources

Developed restoration methods for utility feeders using grid-forming and grid-following inverters, synchronization-aware switching, dynamic microgrid formation, and demand flexibility. Related work includes methods for Connexus Energy and United Power feeder models.

05

Voltage sensitivity and decentralized control

Distribution grids · Smart inverters · Optimization

Developed analytical estimation methods for voltage, current, and loss sensitivities in unbalanced distribution systems, alongside decentralized voltage-control algorithms for distributed energy resources.

06

Building energy predictive control

Buildings · IoT · Model predictive control

Built cloud-enabled predictive control for building energy systems integrating HVAC, storage, solar PV, and loads, with connected building sensors and energy-management systems.
